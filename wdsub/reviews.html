Song reviews rock and alt. rock

Red HotChili Peppers

Californication- This song is a very good song Anthony Kiedis has a way of explaining the true world and understanding it. I think that the song tells the reality of life in cold hard truth most cannot accept.

Nirvana

Heart Shaped Box- This song is also another one of my favorites and i definitely miss Kurt Cobain. He was an awesome song writer and an amazing person. This song is a raw exploration of intense, tumultuous love, addiction, and confinement

My Chemical Romance

The Black Parade- This song is one of my favorites from My Chemical Romance, the song conveys the journey from life to the afterlife, dealing with grief, and finding hope, resilience, and acceptence amidst despair.

Blink 182

I Miss You-I also love this song very much its very soothing and also one my favorite bands/ rock bands. This song is a somber, emotional ballad exploring the vulnerability, regret, and haunting pain of a love that has faded or is falling apart.

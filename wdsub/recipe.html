Recipe for blueberry muffins

  1. Ingredients

    • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
      1. 1/2 cup of sugar
        1. 1 tablespoon baking powder
          1. 1/2 teaspoon of salt
            1. 1 cup of milk
              1. 1/2 cup of butter, melted(1 stick)
                1. 1 large egg
                  1. 1 teaspoon of vanilla
                  2. Dried blueberrys or fresh

                2. Items needed

                  1. Muffin Pan
                  2. Muffin paper cups
                  3. Measuring cup
                  4. Measuring tools
                  5. Wire whisk
                  6. Rubber spatula
                  7. Mixing bowls
                  8. Toothpicks(to check the center of muffin)
                  9. Cooking spray,Pam,or butter.

                3. Instructions

                  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees.
                  2. grease,spray,or put in muffin paper cups
                  3. In a medium bowl,combine flour,sugar,baking powder,and salt
                  4. In a large bowl,with a fork,beat the milk,melted butter,egg,and vanilla until blendid.(careful to add milk and butter before egg so you dont cook the egg)
                  5. Add the flour mixture to the wet mmixture;stir just until flour is moistened(batter will be lumpy)
                  6. Fill the muffin tin cups 1/3 full. Drop 1 rounded teaspoon preserves in the center of each cup batter. Top with the the remaining batter.
                  7. Bake 20-25 minutes. Immediately remove from the pan to a cooloing rack or counter. Serve warm.
